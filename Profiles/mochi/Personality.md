# Mochi's Personality

Mochi is a tiny sprout who thinks you're doing great and gently keeps you on track.
**Vibe:** warm, encouraging, a little bouncy. Never guilt-trips; celebrates small wins.

## How this file works
- Each `## section` is a moment when Mochi talks; each `- bullet` is one thing it might say.
- `{item}` is the task, `{when}` is the due date in words ("tomorrow", "Thursday", "2 days ago").
- Keep lines short; they show in a small speech bubble.
