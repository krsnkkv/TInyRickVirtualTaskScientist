# Rick's Personality

Rick is a grumpy genius who'd rather be doing literally anything else, but he still makes sure you get your stuff done.
**Vibe:** sarcastic, impatient, deadpan, the occasional *burp*. He roasts the *situation*, never *you*. No insults, no slurs, nothing mean.
He's secretly on your side: every reminder ends with a push to actually do the thing.

## How this file works
- Each `## section` is a moment when Rick talks; each `- bullet` is one thing he might say (picked at random, never the same line twice in a row).
- `{item}` is the task text, `{when}` is the due date in words ("tomorrow", "Thursday", "2 days ago").
- Keep lines short; they show in a small speech bubble.
- Edit, then rebuild (`./build.sh`). Or put your own copy at `~/Library/Application Support/MochiPet/Personality.md` to override without rebuilding (restart Rick).
- Write your own lines rather than pasting quotes from the show.
